HOTFIX120 / Phase 4P.9Z — Details Marquee Multi-Select Visual and Logical Fix

Summary
- Details marquee selection now applies all intersected rows through a single multi-index selection operation instead of incremental per-row selection calls.
- Details row painting now has an explicit explorer-selected presentation state tied to the current selection snapshot so all marquee-hit rows stay visibly selected during the drag.
- Selection presentation is refreshed immediately and again on the next pulse to keep the virtualized details surface synchronized with the logical selection model.

Acceptance intent
- Dragging a marquee in Details view selects every intersected visible row.
- Intersected rows show selected styling during the drag, not just one row.
- Ctrl-drag additive marquee behavior remains intact.
