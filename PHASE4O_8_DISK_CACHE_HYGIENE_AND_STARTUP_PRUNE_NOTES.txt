Phase 4O.8 hardens the new safe document disk cache with hygiene and self-healing.

Scope
- deferred startup prune so stale cache files are cleaned even before the next write burst
- self-healing cache reads: empty/corrupt cached PNGs are deleted on read failure
- best-effort clearPersistentDiskCache() utility for future troubleshooting/reset flows
- extra diagnostics for prune runs, startup prune runs, and corrupt cache deletions

Tuning
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.startupPrune.enabled=true|false
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.startupPrune.delayMs=<ms>
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.deleteCorruptOnRead=true|false
