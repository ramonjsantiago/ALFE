HOTFIX30 / Phase 4K.2A — Details Hover Root-Cause Elimination

Included:
- moved selection-state-tokens.css into the critical stylesheet attach path so Details hover/selection colors resolve before first interaction
- removed the late idle re-attach of selection-state-tokens.css to avoid a second mid-session Details row style pass
- localized Details hover/selection lookup colors inside details-view-parity.css so Details rows no longer depend on a later stylesheet for paint conversion
- explicitly neutralized generic TableView hover/selected row rules and child table-cell backgrounds inside explorer-details-table

Why this patch exists:
- HOTFIX29 improved row ownership, but the lingering flicker was still being driven by a root-cause timing bug: details-view-parity.css referenced selection tokens that were not attached until the idle deferred stylesheet phase.
- That late attachment caused JavaFX to evaluate unresolved looked-up colors as Strings during hover, which matches the ClassCastException in the startup log.
- HOTFIX30 fixes the timing and removes the Details view dependency on late token availability.
