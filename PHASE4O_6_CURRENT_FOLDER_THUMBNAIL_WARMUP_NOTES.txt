Phase 4O.6 adds a bounded current-folder thumbnail warmup pass.
After folder navigation settles, the controller warms only the current folder's visible/top thumbnail candidates using background-priority requests,
reusing the existing in-memory cache, coalescing, throttling, and fail-open pipeline.
Warmup is canceled/restarted on navigation and can be tuned with
-Dfileexplorer.thumb.warmup.enabled=true|false,
-Dfileexplorer.thumb.warmup.delayMs=<ms>, and
-Dfileexplorer.thumb.warmup.maxItems=<count>.
