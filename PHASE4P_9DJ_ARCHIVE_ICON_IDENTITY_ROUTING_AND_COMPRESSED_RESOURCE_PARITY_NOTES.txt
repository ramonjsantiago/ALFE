HOTFIX208 / Phase 4P.9DJ
Archive Icon Identity Routing and Compressed Resource Parity

Summary
- Corrected `IconLoader` archive resource routing so `IconType.ARCHIVE` resolves to the packaged `compressed-*.png` icon set.
- Added explicit archive identity override coverage for `kind:archive` and compressed-file extensions `zip`, `7z`, `rar`, `tar`, `gz`, `bz2`, `xz`, and `zst`.
- Eliminated fallback-to-generic behavior for compressed files caused by the old `archive-*.png` lookup path.
