Phase 4O.9 improves thumbnail cache supportability and reset control without changing the normal lazy render path.

Scope
- adds an optional one-shot startup disk-cache reset for troubleshooting stale-cache issues
- keeps the normal startup prune path, now scheduled through a single maintenance pass
- adds multi-line thumbnail diagnostics snapshots with cache directory, size, file count, counters, and effective gates
- includes thumbnail diagnostics in generated support bundle zips

Tuning
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.clearOnStartup=true|false
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.startupPrune.enabled=true|false
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.startupPrune.delayMs=<ms>
- -Dfileexplorer.thumb.diskCache.deleteCorruptOnRead=true|false
